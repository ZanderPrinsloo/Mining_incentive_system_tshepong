Bonus Policy Simulator

Model the cost impact of changing production bonus rules before implementing them. The Efficiency Bonus is driven by the actual policy levers (entry level, netting, B-Reef stope-width, safety %) recomputed per gang from its real m²/labour/safety record — not a flat % of what was paid. Driller and Sweeping Penalty remain simple rate-change sliders. See the impact on total payout by section, save proposals for comparison, and export for review.

How this model works
All figures are anchored to real payroll (PARTICIPANTSDETAIL), not a production-formula estimate: the grand total below always matches the Stope Breaking total on the Bonus Analysis tab exactly for the same period/section — earlier versions of this tool used GANGPRODUCTIONDETAIL's own bonus columns directly, which are a per-person-rate × average-headcount estimate that measurably diverged from what was actually paid (a 15.6% headcount-basis gap was found in one real check). Driller and Safety are taken straight from real payroll; Efficiency and Sweep (bundled together in payroll as one "STM" figure) are split back apart using GANGPRODUCTIONDETAIL's own efficiency:sweep ratio, since only it has the per-gang levers needed to model policy changes. At the policy defaults, Current and Proposed are identical, to the cent — no formula runs at all until you change a lever; this tab just shows the real anchored numbers. The moment you change entry level / netting % / SW % / safety %, the change is modelled by decomposing the anchored Efficiency Bonus into a lever-independent base (using the real netting flag and B-Reef stope-width factor, both verified to multiply exactly onto the pre-anchor estimate) and recomputing forward — from that point on you're looking at a model, not a second real number, so small approximations can appear (e.g. Safety is projected as safety% × the newly-projected Efficiency Bonus, a relationship confirmed on the pre-anchor estimate but not independently re-verified on the anchored figures). No m² tier escalator — the older policy revision this simulator was originally built against had one (an extra 5-50% for breaking 300m²+), but the current policy (Thibakotsi Stoping Incentive Scheme, REV04, effective March 2026) has removed it, and checking real payout ratios across 39 gangs from 100m² to 600m² confirms no tier effect exists in the live data either — so it's gone from this model too. Steep Stope (dip ≥35° adds 10-20% to a panel's m², REV04 §5.10-5.11) is real and active, but it adjusts the square-metre figure before the bonus lookup rather than the Rand amount after — so it's already reflected in every m² and bonus figure in this dashboard, and isn't something this simulator can offer as a separate toggle. Gangs currently earning R0 (below the entry level, or a fatal-incident gang) have no recoverable base and stay at R0 regardless of these levers — see the note above the levers for the count. Driller and Sweeping Penalty are still simple % sliders on the actual paid amount, since we don't have a verified rate breakdown for those components.

Netting and B-Reef stope-width are verified directly against real gang payouts (including the Wide Raise exemption from the entry level), and are explicitly named in the current written policy (REV04 §5.12-5.13). The Safety % ladder works differently: real gang data shows there's no single trustworthy "% of what" to build a ratio on (real "clean" gangs run anywhere from ~20% to ~33% of STM depending on their sweep penalty). So each Safety % lever instead adds the point-change you dial in (new % minus that gang's own real %) directly onto that gang's own real STM and real safety figure — applying only to gangs that actually earned/incurred that tier's bonus or penalty, sized to what they actually got, and moving Total Bonus by exactly that increment (no change at the lever's own default). The older policy revision this simulator was originally built against had an additional m² tier escalator (an extra 5-50% for breaking 300m²+) — the current policy (REV04) has removed that clause, and checking real payout ratios confirms no tier effect exists in the data either, so it's not modelled here. Steep Stope (REV04 §5.10-5.11) is real and active but adjusts the m² figure before the bonus lookup, not the Rand amount after, so it's already reflected in every figure here rather than being a separate lever.
